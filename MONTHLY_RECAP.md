## Personality & Derived Tag Standards — Monthly

### 🎯 Listening Style Tag <sup>(7)</sup>

_Based on — Daily Average_

| Tag                 | Condition            | Headline                    | Text                                                                           |
| ------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Marathoner**      | Daily avg ≥ 90 mins  | _You're a Marathoner._      | You don't just listen — you commit. Long sessions, deep focus, full immersion. |
| **Steady Listener** | Daily avg 45–89 mins | _You're a Steady Listener._ | Not too much, not too little. You've found your rhythm and you stick to it.    |
| **Sprinter**        | Daily avg 15–44 mins | _You're a Sprinter._        | Short, sharp, intentional. You make every minute count.                        |
| **Occasional**      | Daily avg < 15 mins  | _You're an Occasional._     | Life got busy this month — but you still showed up when it mattered.           |
